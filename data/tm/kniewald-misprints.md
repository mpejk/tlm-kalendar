## Suspected misprints in Kniewald's text (kept as printed)

Noticed while the passages were read against the page scans; shown on the site exactly as printed.

- Ash Wednesday, blessing of ashes (Kniewald 1930, pp. 150–151): «ljubažljivo»; «Bože koji» without a comma; the responsory repeat reads first «Pogledaj, Gospode», then «Pogledaj nas, Gospode».
- Easter Vigil, eighth prophecy (Kniewald 1942, p. 285): «po svoj sinajskoj gori» where the Latin has *montis Sion*.
- Easter Vigil, blessing of the font (Kniewald 1942, pp. 291–293): «Tvojih otajstva»; «ove voda».
- Easter Vigil, litany (Kniewald 1942, p. 296): «usliši naš!» in the petition for kings.
- Easter Vigil, Sicut cervus (Kniewald 1942, p. 290): reference printed «Ps. 41, 2, 14» (verses 2–4).
- Common of a martyr bishop, Epistle (Kniewald 1942, p. 789): reference printed «(1, 12-13)» though the whole pericope Jac 1, 12-18 follows.
- Mass *Laetabitur*, introit (Kniewald 1942, p. 795): psalm verse cited as «(Ps. 63, 1)» (Vulgate 63, 2).
