The exact membership of items 3 and 4 cannot be read. This affects items 2 (2035) and 5 (2033) below.
- **n. 95 first sentence is truncated** ("The right of transfer … accidentally impeded by an Office of higher rank, are either … to the rubrics"). The sense (only I class feasts are transferred; others are commemorated or omitted) is supported by n. 96 and n. 93 but the sentence itself is not legible.
- **n. 17a** has lost its lower bound ("Sunday between … and 5th January"); the calendar page supplies it: "Sunday between the octave-day of Christmas and the Epiphany, or if there is none, on 2nd January".
- **The Table of Occurrence** (after the calendar) is unreadable and was not used.

General rules used throughout:

- Sunday I class outranks all feasts except the Immaculate Conception (n. 15).
- Sunday II class outranks II class feasts, but a feast of the Lord of I or II class replaces it with no commemoration (n. 16a); I class feasts outrank it (n. 91 order, commentary on n. 16).
- Ferias I class (Ash Wednesday, all of Holy Week) outrank all feasts (n. 23).
- An impeded I class feast goes to the nearest following day that is not I or II class (n. 96); the Annunciation after Easter goes to Monday after Low Sunday (n. 96a); All Souls on a Sunday goes to Monday (n. 96b).
- A commemoration of a Sunday or of a Lenten feria is privileged (n. 109a, e). On I class days only one privileged commemoration is allowed (n. 111a). On other II class days one commemoration, privileged or ordinary (n. 111c). On a II class Sunday only a II class feast (n. 111b). Excess is omitted (n. 114).
- Ordinary commemorations are made only in low and conventual Masses (n. 108).
- A transferred feast is not commemorated on its original day (nn. 93, 96).

## 1. Annunciation impeded

| Year | 25 March is | Day celebrated on 25 March | Annunciation goes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 | Holy Thursday (Easter 28 Mar) | Holy Thursday, I class (n. 91 item 2, Triduum sacrum) | **Mon 5 April 2027** |
| 2032 | Holy Thursday (Easter 28 Mar) | Holy Thursday, I class | **Mon 5 April 2032** |
| 2029 | Palm Sunday / II Sunday of Passiontide (Easter 1 Apr) | Sunday I class (nn. 11c, 15) | **Mon 9 April 2029** |
| 2035 | Easter Sunday | Easter, I class with octave (n. 91 item 1) | **Mon 2 April 2035** |

- The nearest free day would fall after Easter in every case (Holy Week ferias are I class, n. 23; Easter octave days are I class, n. 66; Low Sunday is I class, n. 11e), so n. 96a applies: Monday after Low Sunday "as to its proper place".
- No commemoration of the Annunciation on 25 March.
- On the transfer day the feast is I class (n. 99). The occurring III class feast is omitted, since an ordinary commemoration is not allowed on a I class day (n. 111a, n. 114): St Vincent Ferrer on 5 April 2027 and 2032, St Francis of Paola on 2 April 2035. Nothing occurs on 9 April 2029 as far as the given data shows. Easter-time ferias are IV class and never commemorated (n. 26).

## 2. St Joseph impeded

| Year | 19 March is | Day celebrated | St Joseph goes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2028 | III Sunday of Lent (Ash Wed 1 Mar) | Sunday I class (nn. 11b, 15) | **Mon 20 March 2028** |
| 2034 | IV Sunday of Lent (Ash Wed 22 Feb) | Sunday I class | **Mon 20 March 2034** |
| 2035 | Monday of Holy Week | Feria I class (n. 23) | **Tue 3 April 2035** |

- 2028, 2034: Monday 20 March is a III class Lenten feria (n. 25a), so it is the nearest day "not I or II class" (n. 96). St Joseph is I class there (n. 99) with a commemoration of the Lenten feria, which is privileged (n. 109e) and allowed (n. 111a). No commemoration of St Joseph on the Sunday.
- 2035: every day from 19 March to Low Sunday 1 April is I class (nn. 23, 66, 11e). The Annunciation is fixed on Monday 2 April by n. 96a "as to its proper place", which makes that Monday a I class day; St Joseph therefore takes the next day that is not I or II class, **Tuesday 3 April 2035** (nothing in the calendar that day; St Isidore is on the 4th). St Francis of Paola (2 April) is omitted (n. 111a).
- **Not fully settled by the text.** n. 98 says that among transferred I class feasts the table order is kept and "in cases of equality, the Office first impeded takes precedence". Both feasts appear to belong to item 11 (illegible, see section 0), and St Joseph is impeded first. Read alone, that would put St Joseph on 2 April and the Annunciation on 3 April. I take n. 96a as the more specific rule: the Monday is the Annunciation's own seat, not merely a transfer under n. 96, and n. 98 speaks of feasts "on successive days", which 19 and 25 March are not. Result: Annunciation 2 April, St Joseph 3 April. This is an interpretation, not a reading.

## 3. 8 December 2030

Advent I is 1 December 2030, so 8 December is the **II Sunday of Advent**. The Immaculate Conception (I class) is celebrated, by the explicit exception in n. 15. Commemoration of the Sunday, privileged (n. 109a, n. 111a). Nothing transferred.

## 4. All Souls on a Sunday

- **2 Nov 2031 and 2 Nov 2036** are both the XXII Sunday after Pentecost (Pentecost 1 June in both years). The Sunday, II class, is celebrated (n. 16b; Changes n. 6b). No commemoration of All Souls.
- All Souls is transferred to **Monday 3 November** in both years (n. 96b), I class (n. 99), with its three Masses (n. 403).

## 5. Nativity of St John the Baptist

**24 June 2033 (Friday).** Verified: Pentecost 5 June, II Sunday after Pentecost 19 June, the Friday after is 24 June, so it is the Sacred Heart.

- Two I class feasts on one day: the higher in the table is kept and the other transferred (n. 97). The Sacred Heart is celebrated on 24 June, I class, without commemoration.
- St John is transferred to the nearest following day not I or II class (n. 96): **Saturday 25 June 2033**, I class (n. 99). St William (III class) is omitted that year (n. 111a, n. 114).
- The vigil on 23 June is **omitted entirely**, because the feast it precedes is transferred (n. 33). Thursday 23 June is then a IV class feria.
- **Caveat.** That the Sacred Heart stands above St John depends on the illegible part of n. 91. Item 3 begins "Feasts of Epiphany, Ascension, Blessed Trinity, Corpus …" and the list of I class feasts puts the Sacred Heart directly after Corpus Christi among the feasts of the Lord, while St John is in the residual group. I am confident of the order but it cannot be read off the page.

**24 June 2029 (V Sunday after Pentecost) and 24 June 2035 (VI Sunday after Pentecost).** St John, I class, outranks a II class Sunday (n. 91; n. 16 limits the Sunday's precedence to II class feasts). Commemoration of the Sunday, privileged (n. 109a, n. 111a). The vigil on Saturday 23 June is kept, II class; it outranks the Saturday Office of Our Lady (n. 31) and n. 33 does not apply.

## 7. Earliest and latest Easter

- **Easter 25 March 2035** confirmed, the earliest in the range. Consequences are in sections 1 and 2. 25 April 2035 is an ordinary Wednesday, so St Mark and the Greater Litanies are undisturbed.
- **Easter 21 April 2030** confirmed, the latest in the range. 25 April 2030 is **Thursday of Easter week**, I class (n. 66).
  - St Mark (II class) is impeded. It is not transferred, since only I class feasts are (n. 96; n. 95 damaged). It is not commemorated either, being an ordinary commemoration on a I class day (n. 111a). **St Mark is omitted in 2030.**
  - The Greater Litanies stay on 25 April. They move to Tuesday only when Easter Sunday or Easter Monday is 25 April (n. 80; commentary: they "cannot be transferred from the days fixed"). They are commemorated in the Mass of Easter Thursday, a privileged commemoration (n. 109f), which is the one allowed on a I class day (n. 111a).
  - Where the procession is held, the Rogation Mass is a votive Mass II class (n. 346), which a I class day impedes. The Mass of the day takes its place and counts as part of the rite (n. 347).

## 8. Two further cases

**Vigil of St Lawrence (9 August) on a Saturday** (2031 and 2036 in this range). The vigil prevails. It is III class and "takes precedence over liturgical days IV class" (n. 32; n. 91 item 26 above item 27). The Saturday Office of Our Lady exists only where a IV class feria occurs (n. 78). Mass of the vigil with commemoration of St Romanus, ordinary (n. 111d). Our Lady is not commemorated.

**27 December 2026 (Sunday).** The **Sunday within the octave of Christmas**, II class, is celebrated, with commemoration of St John (n. 69: "the Office of the Sunday is always celebrated with a commemoration of any feast that may occur … unless the Sunday falls on a feast I class"). St John is II class (n. 68b) and a II class feast is the one commemoration a II class Sunday admits (n. 111b). No separate commemoration of the octave (n. 112b).

## Points not settled by the text

1. **n. 91 items 3–12 are illegible.** The Sacred Heart ranking above St John the Baptist (24 June 2033) and the Annunciation and St Joseph sharing one item rest on fragments and cross-references.
2. **2035 order of the Annunciation and St Joseph.** I give 2 April and 3 April by n. 96a; n. 98's "first impeded" clause could be read the other way.
3. **St Sixtus on 6 August and St Theodore on 9 November when the feast replaces a Sunday.** Kept as ordinary commemorations under n. 111c; n. 16a with n. 111b could be read to exclude them.
4. **n. 95 is truncated.** That II class feasts such as St Mark are never transferred is inferred from n. 96 and n. 93.
5. **Commemoration of the octave on St Stephen (26 December).** It follows from nn. 109c and 111c, but n. 70 defers the detail to the Missal, which is not in this source.
