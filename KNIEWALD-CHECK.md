# Kniewald check

Generated by `npx tsx scripts/kniewald/check.ts`. Do not edit by hand (the "Not found" explanation comes from `scripts/kniewald/not-found-categories.md`).

## Method

- Old-site side: every block of `data/tm/blocks.json` (the Croatian cell as printed on tradicionalnamisa.com). Reference lines, inline references, bracketed notes, "Pomolimo se", the prayer conclusions, "Slava Ocu" and the repeated introit antiphon are removed before comparing.
- Kniewald side: *Rimski misal za privatnu upotrebu*, 4th ed. 1942, archive.org OCR (`rimski_misal_za_privatnu_upotrebu_1942-dragutin_kniewald`), one OCR paragraph per line. Line-end hyphenation is joined, running heads and page numbers are dropped, and paragraphs are grouped into labelled units (Ulaz, Molitva, Čitanje, Na stepenicama / Zavlaka / Aleluja, Evanđelje, Prikazanje, Tajna, Pričesna, Popričesna, Molitva nad pukom, Predslovlje), with continuation across page breaks. Kniewald's abbreviations ("po G. n.", "Smiluj mi se . . .") are removed in the same way.
- Matching: words are lower-cased, stripped of diacritics, folded for the usual OCR confusions (l/1/i/j) and cut to four letters; candidates of the same kind of section come from an inverted index and are ranked by IDF-weighted overlap (F-measure), with bonuses for a shared chapter:verse reference, an identical citation, and nearness to the other texts of the same old-site page.
- Content comparison, not wording. Readings and Gospels: a longest-common-subsequence alignment gives the unmatched material at the head and tail of each side (pericope boundaries) and the largest one-sided gap in the middle; the printed citations (chapter and verses) are compared as well. Prayers and chants: only the extent (length ratio with high containment) is checked, since the old site's Croatian is a later revision and rewording is expected. A section printed in several rows (gradual + alleluia or tract, several lessons) is judged row by row.
- A difference where the *OCR passage* is the shorter side is not held against the old site when the citations are identical or the OCR paragraph visibly breaks off; those are counted under "citation only" or "uncertain".
- **The Kniewald text in `data/tm/kniewald-alt.json` is raw OCR, uncorrected** (č ć đ š ž and single letters are often wrong, some passages are cut at page breaks). It must be corrected against the page scans before it is displayed. Its `status` uses `uncertain` also for the "citation only" and "by rows" cases. Page references give the two printed pages of the scan spread and the scan number ("snimka").
