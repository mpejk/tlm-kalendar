# Kniewald check

Generated by `npx tsx scripts/kniewald/check.ts`. Do not edit by hand.

## Method

- Old-site side: every block of `data/tm/blocks.json` (the Croatian cell as printed on tradicionalnamisa.com). Reference lines, inline references, bracketed notes, "Pomolimo se", the prayer conclusions, "Slava Ocu" and the repeated introit antiphon are removed before comparing.
- Kniewald side: *Rimski misal za privatnu upotrebu*, 4th ed. 1942, archive.org OCR (`rimski_misal_za_privatnu_upotrebu_1942-dragutin_kniewald`), one OCR paragraph per line. Line-end hyphenation is joined, running heads and page numbers are dropped, and paragraphs are grouped into labelled units (Ulaz, Molitva, Čitanje, Na stepenicama / Zavlaka / Aleluja, Evanđelje, Prikazanje, Tajna, Pričesna, Popričesna, Molitva nad pukom, Predslovlje). Kniewald's abbreviations ("po G. n.", "Smiluj mi se . . .") are removed in the same way.
- Matching: words are lower-cased, stripped of diacritics, folded for the usual OCR confusions (l/1/i/j) and cut to four letters; candidates come from an inverted index, ranked by IDF-weighted overlap (F-measure), with bonuses for the same kind of section, a shared chapter:verse reference and nearness to the other texts of the same old-site page.
- Content comparison, not wording: for texts of 30+ words (readings, Gospels, long chants) a longest-common-subsequence alignment gives the unmatched material at the head and tail of each side (pericope boundaries) and the largest one-sided gap in the middle; for short texts only the length ratio is checked. The old site's Croatian is a later revision, so rewording is expected and is not flagged.
- **The Kniewald text in `data/tm/kniewald-alt.json` is raw OCR, uncorrected** (č ć đ š ž and single letters are often wrong). It must be corrected against the page scans before it is displayed. Page references give the two printed pages of the scan spread and the scan number ("snimka").
