# Blocked

- **Latin of the Croatian proper prayers.** Kniewald 1942 has, in Croatian only, proper prayers for St Ladislaus (p. 612), St Demetrius (p. 730), Bl. Marko Križevčanin (p. 699), Bl. Nikola Tavelić (p. 779), Bl. Augustin Kažotić (p. 666), Bl. Ozana (p. 553), Bl. Gracija (pp. 772–773) and the full proper Mass of the Holy Relics on 5 November (pp. 770–771). The Latin originals (the *Proprium* of Zagreb, or the *Missae pro aliquibus locis* for the Holy Relics) are not in the Divinum Officium data and were not found on archive.org (searched 16 Nov 2026). Since no liturgical text may be written from memory, these feasts use the prayers of the Common. To use the proper texts: add the Latin to `data/corpus/overrides.yaml` / a formulary in `data/local/formularies.yaml`, the Croatian from the Kniewald pages above to `data/tm/kniewald-found.json`, and rebuild.
- **Pre-conciliar Zagreb *Proprium* / *Directorium*.** Not found online; ranks therefore follow the Code of Rubrics and the mapping in the brief.
- **Firefox (review task, Nov 2026).** The reviewer asked for the day, month, year and search views to be run in Firefox as well. Playwright's Firefox (build 1572) downloads but does not start on this Mac (macOS 27.2, "Could not find profile folder", also outside the sandbox). The checks ran in Chromium and WebKit (140 layout cases, light and dark, 100/130/160 % type, 340 px); the scripts under `scripts/verify/site-*.ts` run Firefox automatically where it starts and report it as skipped where it does not. Nothing was tested on a physical iPhone either; the font fault was reproduced and cleared in Playwright WebKit with an iPhone profile and by shaping the font files directly.
