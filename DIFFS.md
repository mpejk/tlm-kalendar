### Exceptions and their causes

- St Stephen: the old site says the Creed is not said; it is said on every day of the Christmas octave (Code of Rubrics n. 475d). The calendar follows the rubric. — pages: 2016-12-26_0, 2017-12-26_0, 2018-12-26_0
- Old-site typo (eloquiumvtum for eloquium tuum) in the offertory of Friday and Saturday after Ash Wednesday; corrected against Divinum Officium. — pages: 2017-03-03_0, 2017-03-04_0, 2018-02-16_0, 2018-02-17_0
- Old-site typos (qual, offerimum) in the secret of the 18th Sunday after Pentecost; corrected against Divinum Officium. — pages: 2017-10-15_0, 2018-09-30_0
- Old site labels the single prophetic lesson of some Ember Fridays Epístola and of others Léctio; the calendar uses Léctio whenever the reading is not from an Epistle. — pages: 2017-03-10_0, 2018-02-23_0, 2018-02-24_0, 2018-09-21_0
- Rogation Mass: the old site prints the Paschal Alleluia of the votive Mass for any necessity; the Missal's Rogation Mass has Alleluia. Confitemini Domino (Divinum Officium), which is kept. — pages: 2017-04-25_1, 2017-05-22_1, 2017-05-23_1, 2017-05-24_1, 2018-04-25_1, 2018-05-07_1, 2018-05-08_1, 2018-05-09_1
- St Joseph the Worker: the old site has the Croatian postcommunion in the Latin column; Latin from Divinum Officium. — pages: 2017-05-01_0, 2018-05-01_0
- Ascension: the old site's rank line says Nedjelja (Sunday); it is a feast of I class. — pages: 2017-05-25_0, 2018-05-10_0
- Queenship of Our Lady: for the commemoration of St Petronilla the old site repeats the secret of the feast; the calendar gives St Petronilla's own secret. — pages: 2017-05-31_0
- St Augustine: the old site prints the verse Inveni David without Alleluia; Divinum Officium has Alleluia. Amavit eum. Kept from Divinum Officium (listed for review). — pages: 2017-08-28_0, 2018-08-28_0
- Maternity of Our Lady: the old site omits the word Alleluia around the verse; text otherwise the same. — pages: 2017-10-11_0, 2018-10-11_0
- Sts Simon and Jude: the old site prints the Alleluia verse and Gospel of St James (Mt 20); the Missal has Nimis honorati and Jn 15, 17-25 (Divinum Officium). — pages: 2017-10-28_0
- Chair of St Peter: the old site continues the offertory with 'et quodcumque ligaveris…'; the Missal's antiphon ends at 'claves regni caelorum' (Divinum Officium). — pages: 2017-02-22_0, 2018-02-22_0
- St Francis Caracciolo: old site 'sicut cedrus Libani', Missal 'sicut cedrus, quae in Libano est' (Divinum Officium). — pages: 2018-06-04_0
- Saturday Mass of Our Lady in Paschaltide: the old site prints the antiphon three times. — pages: 2017-05-06_0
- Holy Thursday evening Mass: the old site inserts the washing of the feet (antiphons) after the Gospel; the calendar shows the Mandatum with the rites of Holy Week. — pages: 2017-04-13_1, 2018-03-29_1

Latin taken from the old site instead of Divinum Officium (where the old site has the text of the 1962 Missal): see `data/corpus/la/OVERRIDES.md`.
